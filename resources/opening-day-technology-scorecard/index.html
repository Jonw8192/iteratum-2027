Opening-Day Technology Scorecard

A practical GREEN / YELLOW / RED assessment for answering one question before a new location opens: can this location actually operate?

How to Use the Scorecard

Score evidence, not optimism.

Review each category against the opening date. Ask for evidence that the work is installed, configured, tested, owned, and recoverable if something slips. Then assign the status that reflects the location today.

GREEN — Ready

Installed, configured, tested, and on track. Ownership is clear and no opening-critical gap is unresolved.

YELLOW — Action Required

Something is incomplete or at risk, but there is a credible corrective path with an owner and enough time to act.

RED — Opening Risk

A missing dependency, delay, failed test, or unresolved decision creates a credible risk to opening-day operations.

Use One Owner and One Date

Every YELLOW or RED item should have a named owner, next action, and due date.

The score is useful only if it drives action. Do not let “waiting on vendor” become a status. Record who is accountable for the next move and when the item will be tested again.

The Scorecard

Ten categories that have to come together before the doors open.

Use the prompts below as a readiness review. They are deliberately operational: the goal is not to prove equipment exists, but to prove the location can use it.

Internet & WAN

Primary connectivity is installed and active, required circuit details are known, and backup connectivity has a tested or documented plan.

  • Carrier order and install date confirmed
  • Primary circuit active and tested
  • Static/public IP requirements confirmed where needed
  • Backup/failover path defined and tested where applicable
  • Escalation owner and carrier contact known

Network & Wi-Fi

The network is not just mounted—it is configured, reachable, segmented as required, and validated in the actual space.

  • Firewall, switching, and access points installed
  • Required cabling/drops terminated and tested
  • VLANs or segmentation configured where required
  • Wi-Fi coverage and business-critical devices tested
  • Remote management and support access confirmed

POS / Booking

The location-specific operating system is installed, configured, licensed, connected, and ready for a real workflow test.

  • Hardware received and installed
  • Location configuration complete
  • Printers and required peripherals mapped
  • Required integrations and licenses active
  • Test order, booking, or customer workflow completed

Payments

Payment readiness is proved with an actual transaction—not inferred from terminals being onsite.

  • Merchant/location setup active
  • Payment terminals paired and configured
  • Gateway or processor connection working
  • Successful test transaction completed
  • Settlement or transaction visibility confirmed

05

Cameras & Security

Security systems are online, recording, accessible to the right people, and no longer waiting on another technology dependency.

  • Cameras/security devices installed and online
  • Recording verified
  • Remote or management access tested
  • Network and power dependencies cleared
  • Provider handoff and ownership confirmed

06

Phones

The location can receive and place calls using the correct numbers and expected call flow.

  • Numbers assigned or ported
  • Phones installed and provisioned
  • Inbound and outbound calls tested
  • Voicemail/call routing confirmed
  • Location and emergency information verified where required
GYR
07

Users & Access

Managers and employees can sign in to the systems they need with the right permissions before opening-day pressure begins.

  • Required accounts created
  • Permissions and roles assigned
  • MFA or authentication setup complete
  • Email and required applications accessible
  • Manager/admin access tested
GYR
08

Devices & SaaS

Opening-critical computers, tablets, printers, peripherals, and cloud applications are assigned, licensed, connected, and usable.

  • Devices onsite and staged
  • Required applications installed or accessible
  • Licenses/subscriptions active
  • Printers and peripherals tested
  • Ownership for support after opening is clear
GYR
09

Vendors & Dependencies

Every critical handoff has an owner, date, dependency, and escalation route instead of living between vendor scopes.

  • Vendor ownership clear for each critical system
  • Installation dates and lead times confirmed
  • Construction/site dependencies visible
  • Open decisions have owners and deadlines
  • Contingencies exist for credible opening risks
GYR
10

End-to-End Go-Live Test

Prove the systems work together by walking through the operating sequence a real location needs on opening day.

  • Employee logs in successfully
  • Internet and required applications work
  • Customer transaction or booking is created
  • Payment processes and receipt/output completes
  • Camera records, phone receives a call, and manager access works
GYR
Overall Readiness Decision

Do not average away an opening-critical risk.

The scorecard should end with a clear operating decision and a short list of conditions—not a vague percentage.

READY

Technology is ready for opening.

Opening-critical systems have been validated and remaining items do not create a credible operating risk.

READY WITH CONDITIONS

Open items remain, but the path is controlled.

Conditions, owners, due dates, contingencies, and escalation routes are explicit and leadership accepts the remaining risk.

NOT READY

A credible opening-day risk remains unresolved.

The location needs corrective action, a contingency, or a leadership decision before technology readiness can be declared.

Turn the Score Into Action

The useful output is the corrective plan.

For every YELLOW or RED item, write down the action that changes the status—not simply the symptom.

Keep the action log short enough that leadership can scan it and detailed enough that the owner knows exactly what must happen next.
