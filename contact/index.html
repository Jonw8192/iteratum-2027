Before We Talk

Four details make the conversation much more useful.

You do not need a polished project plan. A few operating facts are enough to understand the timing, complexity, and where the opening may be exposed.

If some of these answers are unclear, that is useful information too. The point is to establish the current reality quickly rather than make you complete a long intake form.

01

Opening date

When does the location need to be operational for employees and customers?

02

Current footprint

How many locations are operating today, and how repeatable is the current technology setup?

03

Planned openings

How many openings or major remodels are expected over the next 12 months?

04

Known risks

What is late, uncertain, unowned, untested, or causing the most concern right now?

Best Fit

Most useful for operators with real opening complexity.

Good Fit
  • Growing multi-location operator
  • Multiple technology vendors or providers involved
  • Small or stretched operations / IT team
  • An opening or major remodel approaching
  • Unclear ownership across technology dependencies
  • Need for an independent readiness view before opening day
Probably Not the Right Fit
  • You need onsite cabling or equipment installation
  • You are looking for a managed IT provider or help desk
  • You need a POS reseller or hardware vendor
  • The project has no defined opening, remodel, or rollout outcome
  • You only need routine configuration inside one vendor's product
